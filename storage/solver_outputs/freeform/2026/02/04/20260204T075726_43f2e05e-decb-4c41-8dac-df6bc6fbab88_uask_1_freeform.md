# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x + 1 = 2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- All real numbers are allowed for $x $; no restrictions.

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify The Equation
We have a simple linear equation in one variable: $ x+1=2 $.

## Step 2: Isolate The Variable
Subtract $ 1$ from both sides to move constants: $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Left side simplifies to $ x$; right side simplifies to $1 $.

## Step 4: Conclude The Solution
Thus the equation reduces to $ x=1 $.

## Step 5: State Uniqueness
Linear equation yields a single unique solution $ x=1 $.

## Step 6: Present Result
Solution is $ x=1 $, which satisfies the original equation.

# Graphs
- None

# Verification
- Check 1: Substitute $ x=1 $ into $ x+1 $ gives $ 1+1=2 $, true.
- Check 2: No other real number satisfies the equation.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
