# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- The variable $x $ is a real number; no additional constraints.

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify Equation Type
This is a first-degree linear equation in one variable.

## Step 2: Isolate Variable Term
Subtract $ 1$ from both sides to move constants: $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Compute the arithmetic: $ x=1 $.

## Step 4: State Solution
Conclude that the solution is $ x=1 $.

## Step 5: Check Substitution
Substitute $ x=1 $ into the original equation: $ 1+1=2 $.

## Step 6: Confirm Validity
Since $ 2=2 $ holds, the solution is valid.

# Graphs
- None

# Verification
- Check 1: Substitute $ x=1 $ gives $ 1+1=2 $, true.
- Check 2: Algebraic isolation yields a unique real solution $ x=1 $.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
