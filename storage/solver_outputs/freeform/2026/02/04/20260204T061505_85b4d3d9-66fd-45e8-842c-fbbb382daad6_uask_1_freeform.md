# Recognized Problem
Evaluate the definite integral $\int_0^1 x^2\,dx $.

# Domain Constraints
- None (polynomial integrand, defined on $[0,1]$)

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify antiderivative
The antiderivative of $ x^2 $ is found using the power rule yielding $ F(x)=\tfrac{x^3}{3}$.

## Step 2: Apply Fundamental Theorem
Use the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus to write $\int_0^1 x^2\,dx=F(1)-F(0)$.

## Step 3: Evaluate at upper limit
Compute $F(1)=\tfrac{1^3}{3}=\tfrac{1}{3}$.

## Step 4: Evaluate at lower limit
Compute $F(0)=\tfrac{0^3}{3}=0 $.

## Step 5: Subtract values
Subtract to get $\tfrac{1}{3}-0=\tfrac{1}{3}$.

## Step 6: Conclude result
Conclude that the definite integral equals $\tfrac{1}{3}$.
