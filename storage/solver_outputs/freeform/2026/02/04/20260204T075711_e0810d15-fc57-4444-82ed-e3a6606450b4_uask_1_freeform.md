# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x + 1 = 2 $.

# Domain Constraints
- $ x$ is a real number; equation valid for all real $x $.

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate The Variable
Subtract $ 1$ from both sides to begin isolating $x $.

## Step 2: Perform The Subtraction
Compute $ x + 1 - 1 = 2 - 1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Left side simplifies to $ x$. Right side simplifies to $1 $.

## Step 4: Conclude The Solution
Thus $ x = 1 $ is the solution obtained.

## Step 5: Check The Solution
Substitute $ x=1 $ into original: $ 1 + 1 = 2 $.

## Step 6: Confirm Validity
Since $ 2 = 2 $, the solution is valid in the domain of real numbers.
