# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $.

# Domain Constraints
- $ x$ is a real number; no additional constraints.

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify Equation Type
This is a first-degree linear equation in one variable.

## Step 2: Isolate The Variable
Subtract $1 $ from both sides to isolate $ x$.

## Step 3: Perform The Subtraction
Compute $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 4: Simplify Both Sides
Left side simplifies to $ x$, right side simplifies to $1 $.

## Step 5: State The Solution
Conclude that $ x=1 $.

## Step 6: Reflect On Uniqueness
Linear equation yields a single unique solution in the reals.

# Graphs
- None

# Verification
- Check 1: Substitute $ x=1 $ into $ x+1 $: $ 1+1=2 $ holds.
- Check 2: No other real $ x$ satisfies the equation.

# Final Answer
**Text:** The solution is $x=1 $.
**LaTeX:** $$x=1$$
