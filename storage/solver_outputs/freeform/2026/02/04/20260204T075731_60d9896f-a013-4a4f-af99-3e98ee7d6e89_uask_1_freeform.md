# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $.

# Domain Constraints
- $ x$ is a real number; equation valid for all real $x $.

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate The Variable
Subtract 1 from both sides to begin isolating $ x$.

Applying the subtraction: $$x+1-1=2-1.$$

## Step 2: Simplify Both Sides
Combine like terms on each side.

Left simplifies to $x $, right simplifies to $ 1$.

## Step 3: Conclude The Solution
State the value found for $x $.

Thus $ x=1 $.

## Step 4: Check Substitution
Substitute $ x=1 $ back into the original equation.

Compute $ 1+1=2 $.

## Step 5: Verify Equality Holds
Confirm that both sides match after substitution.

Since $ 2=2 $, the solution is valid.

## Step 6: State Uniqueness
Note uniqueness for linear equation.

A first-degree equation has a single solution: $ x=1 $.
