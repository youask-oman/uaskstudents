# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- The variable $x $ is a real number; no further restrictions.

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify Equation Type
This is a linear equation in one variable of the form $ x+1=2 $.

## Step 2: Isolate Variable Term
Subtract $ 1$ from both sides to move constants: $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Simplify left and right: $ x=1 $.

## Step 4: State Solution
Conclude that the solution is $ x=1 $.

## Step 5: Check Solution Substitution
Substitute $ x=1 $ into original: $ 1+1=2 $, which simplifies to $ 2=2 $.

## Step 6: Confirm Uniqueness
Linear equation yields a single unique solution $ x=1 $ over the reals.
