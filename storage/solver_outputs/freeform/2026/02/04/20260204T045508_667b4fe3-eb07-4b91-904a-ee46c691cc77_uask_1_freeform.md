Step 2: title: Compute product and sum — Compute $ac=1\cdot6=6$ and note we need two numbers that multiply to $6$ and add to $b=-5$.
Step 3: title: Find integer pair — Identify the integers $-2$ and $-3$ since $(-2)(-3)=6$ and $-2+(-3)=-5$.
Step 4: title: Factor quadratic — Factor as $(x-2)(x-3)=0$ using the pair $-2,-3$.
Step 5: title: Apply zero-product property — Set each factor equal to zero giving $x-2=0$ or $x-3=0$.
Step 6: title: Solve for roots — Solve to obtain $x=2$ and $x=3$.
