# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation: x + 1 = 2

# Domain Constraints
- The equation is linear with variable $x $ appearing to the first power. All real numbers are allowed for $ x$ (domain: $x \in \mathbb{R}$).

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify Equation Type
This is a simple linear equation in one variable of the form $x + 1 = 2 $.

## Step 2: Isolate The Variable
To solve for $ x$, perform an operation that isolates $x $ on one side. Subtract $ 1$ from both sides of the equation.

## Step 3: Perform The Subtraction
Subtract $1 $ from each side:
$$ x + 1 - 1 = 2 - 1.$$

## Step 4: Simplify Both Sides
Simplify the left and right expressions:
$$x = 1.$$

## Step 5: State The Solution
The solution obtained by isolating and simplifying is $x = 1 $.

## Step 6: Consider Uniqueness
Because the equation is linear and not degenerate, the solution $ x=1 $ is unique in the real numbers.
