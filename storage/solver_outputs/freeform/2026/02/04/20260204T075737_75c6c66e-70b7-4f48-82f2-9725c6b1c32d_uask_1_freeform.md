# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x + 1 = 2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- The equation is over the real numbers. All real $x $ are allowed.

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate The Variable
Subtract 1 from both sides to begin isolating $ x$.

## Step 2: Apply Subtraction
Compute $x + 1 - 1 = 2 - 1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Left side simplifies to $ x$, right side simplifies to $1 $.

## Step 4: Conclude The Solution
Thus $ x = 1 $.

## Step 5: State Uniqueness
Linear equation yields a single unique solution in reals: $ x=1 $.

## Step 6: Present Final Form
Write the solution explicitly as $ x=1 $.

# Graphs
- None

# Verification
- Check 1: Substitute back: $ 1 + 1 = 2 $ holds.
- Check 2: No other real number satisfies the equation.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
