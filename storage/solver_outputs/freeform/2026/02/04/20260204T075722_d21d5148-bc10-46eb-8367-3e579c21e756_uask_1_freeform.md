# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x + 1 = 2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- Equation is defined for all real numbers; no restrictions on $x $.

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate The Variable
Subtract 1 from both sides to begin isolating $ x$.

Subtracting: $x + 1 - 1 = 2 - 1 $.

## Step 2: Simplify Both Sides
Simplify each side after subtraction.

Left becomes $ x$, right becomes $1 $.

## Step 3: State The Solution
Conclude the value of $ x$ from the simplification.

Thus $x = 1 $.

## Step 4: Check By Substitution
Substitute $ x=1 $ back into the original equation to verify.

Compute $ 1 + 1 = 2 $.

## Step 5: Confirm Equality Holds
Compare substitution result to right-hand side.

Since $ 2 = 2 $, the equality holds.

## Step 6: Conclude Uniqueness
State uniqueness for linear equation.

Linear equation yields single solution $ x=1 $ over reals.
