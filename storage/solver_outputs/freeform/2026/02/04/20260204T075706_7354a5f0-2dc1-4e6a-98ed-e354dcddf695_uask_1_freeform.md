# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x + 1 = 2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- $x $ is a real number; no other restrictions.

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate The Variable
Subtract 1 from both sides to begin isolating $ x$.

## Step 2: Perform The Subtraction
Compute $x + 1 - 1 = 2 - 1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Left side simplifies to $ x$, right side simplifies to $1 $.

## Step 4: State The Result
Conclude that $ x = 1 $ after simplification.

## Step 5: Check The Solution
Substitute $ x=1 $ back into the original equation: $ 1+1=2 $.

## Step 6: Confirm Validity
Since $ 2=2 $ holds, the solution is valid in the real numbers.

# Graphs
- None

# Verification
- Plugging $ x=1 $ yields $ 1+1=2 $, which is true.
- The operation used (subtracting 1) is valid for all real numbers.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
