# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation x+1=2 for x.

# Domain Constraints
- None

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate constant term
Subtract 1 from both sides to move constants: $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 2: Simplify left side
Compute $ x+1-1 $ which simplifies to $ x$.

## Step 3: Simplify right side
Compute $2-1 $ which simplifies to $ 1$.

## Step 4: State resulting equation
Conclude the equation is $x=1 $ after simplification.

## Step 5: Confirm operation validity
Note subtraction of 1 from both sides is valid for all real numbers and preserves equality.

## Step 6: Finalize solution
Therefore the unique solution is $ x=1 $.

# Graphs

# Verification
- Substitute $ x=1 $ into original: $ 1+1=2 $ gives $ 2=2 $, true.
- Domain check: no restrictions, $ x=1 $ is allowed.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
