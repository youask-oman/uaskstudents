# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- All real numbers; equation defined for $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

# Steps
## Step 1: Identify Equation Type
This is a first-degree linear equation in one variable: $x+1=2 $.

## Step 2: Isolate Variable Term
Subtract $ 1$ from both sides to move constant terms: $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify Both Sides
Simplify expressions: $ x=1 $.

## Step 4: Check Substitution
Substitute $ x=1 $ into original: $ 1+1=2 $.

## Step 5: Verify Equality Holds
Compute left side: $ 2$, which equals right side $2 $, so solution valid.

## Step 6: State Solution Uniquely
Conclude the unique solution for the equation is $ x=1 $.
