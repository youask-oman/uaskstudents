# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $.

# Domain Constraints
- None

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate variable term
Subtract $ 1$ from both sides to remove the constant from the left side.

## Step 2: Perform subtraction
Compute $x+1-1=2-1 $ to simplify both sides.

## Step 3: Simplify left side
Use $ 1-1=0 $ so the left side becomes $ x$.

## Step 4: Simplify right side
Compute $2-1=1 $ so the right side becomes $ 1$.

## Step 5: Conclude solution
Equate simplified sides to get $x=1 $.

## Step 6: Present solution
State the solution as $ x=1 $ and prepare for verification.

# Graphs
- None

# Verification
- Substitute $ x=1 $ into $ x+1 $ to get $ 1+1=2 $, which matches the right side.
- Domain is all real numbers, so $ x=1 $ is valid.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
