# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x + 1 = 2 $ for $ x$.

# Domain Constraints
- Equation involves real numbers; domain: $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate The Variable
Subtract 1 from both sides to begin isolating $x $.

## Step 2: Perform The Subtraction
Compute $ x + 1 - 1 = 2 - 1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify The Left Side
Left side simplifies to $ x$ because $+1-1=0 $.

## Step 4: Simplify The Right Side
Right side simplifies to $ 1$ because $2-1=1 $.

## Step 5: State The Solution
Conclude that $ x = 1 $ after simplification.

## Step 6: Confirm Solution Format
Express solution clearly as a single real number.

- None

# Verification
- Check 1: Substitute $ x=1 $ into original equation: $ 1+1=2 $, true.
- Check 2: Solution is within domain $\mathbb{R}$.

# Final Answer
**Text:** x = 1
**LaTeX:** $$x = 1$$
