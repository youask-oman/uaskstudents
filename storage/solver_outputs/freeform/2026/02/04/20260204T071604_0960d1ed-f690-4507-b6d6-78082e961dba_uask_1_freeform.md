# Recognized Problem
Solve the linear equation $x+1=2 $.

# Domain Constraints
- None

# Steps
## Step 1: Isolate variable term
Subtract 1 from both sides to begin isolating $ x$.

## Step 2: Perform subtraction
Compute $x+1-1=2-1 $.

## Step 3: Simplify left side
Simplify $ x+0 $ to get $ x$ on the left.

## Step 4: Simplify right side
Simplify $2-1 $ to get $ 1$ on the right.

## Step 5: State solution
Conclude that $x=1 $ after simplification.

## Step 6: Conclude uniqueness
Note that this linear equation has a single unique solution $ x=1 $.

- Substitute $ x=1 $ into $ x+1 $ to get $ 1+1=2 $, which matches the right side.
