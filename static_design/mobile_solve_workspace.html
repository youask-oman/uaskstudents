Step 3: Normalization Condition

Integrate the squared magnitude of the wave function over the entire width of the box (0 to L) and set it to 1:

∫₀ᴸ |ψ(x)|² dx = 1
∫₀ᴸ A² sin²(nπx/L) dx = 1
A = √(2/L)

Tip: Use the identity sin²θ = (1 - cos 2θ)/2 to solve the integral.
