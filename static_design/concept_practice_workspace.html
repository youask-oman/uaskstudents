Angular Momentum (Point)

\( \vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p} \)

\( \vec{p} = m\vec{v} \) is linear momentum

Rigid Body Rotation

\( L = I\omega \)

\( I \) = Moment of Inertia

Torque Relation

\( \vec{\tau} = \frac{d\vec{L}}{dt} \)
