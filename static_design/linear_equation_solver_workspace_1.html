Step 1: Undo multiplication by 3

The entire expression (x - 2) is being multiplied by 3. To isolate the parenthesis, divide both sides of the equation by 3.

quiz Checkpoint

Why is division the correct first step here?

3(x - 2) / 3 = 15 / 3
x - 2 = 5

Step 2: Undo subtraction of 2

Now that (x - 2) is isolated, add 2 to both sides of the equation to find the value of x.

task_alt Verification Quiz

What happens if we subtract 2 instead of adding?

x - 2 + 2 = 5 + 2
x = 7

Common Mistakes

Dividing only one side

Forgetting to divide the right side (15) by 3 while dividing the left side. Remember: What you do to one side, you must do to the other.

Incorrect Distribution

If distributing, students often forget to multiply 3 by -2, leading to 3x - 2 = 15 instead of 3x - 6 = 15.
