Key Concepts

Algebra Foundation

Slope-Intercept Form

The equation y = mx + b is the slope-intercept form of a linear equation, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.

Rule: If m > 0, the line rises; if m < 0, the line falls.
Linear Geometry

Cartesian Coordinate Plane

Linear equations represent straight lines on a 2D plane. Understanding how coordinates $(x, y)$ satisfy the equation is essential for graphing.

Hint: Every point on the line is a solution to the equation.
