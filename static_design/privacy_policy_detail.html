Model Training & OCR

At uask.ai, we use Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to translate your handwritten or digital math and physics problems into machine-readable formats. This process occurs locally when possible or on our secure cloud servers.

Continuous Improvement: To provide more accurate solutions for complex symbols (like triple integrals or Feynman diagrams), we may use anonymized versions of processed problems to refine our specialized math-LLM models. You can opt-out of model training at any time in your Privacy Settings.
